## Mean and CV breakdown

Run 0 (cold) is retained in the raw data but excluded from warm-run statistics. Each mean is the average across the warm captures. CV (coefficient of variation) is the sample standard deviation divided by the mean, expressed as a percentage. Lower CV means less relative variation between separate captures.

| Metric | Adrenaline mean | Adrenaline CV | Citron mean | Citron CV | Change in mean |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Average FPS | 128.17 | 5.3% | 105.82 | 1.4% | -17.4% |
| P1 FPS | 68.84 | 13.6% | 63.90 | 2.1% | -7.2% |
| 1% low average FPS | 47.66 | 16.3% | 47.00 | 2.4% | -1.4% |
| 0.1% low average FPS | 16.88 | 24.1% | 12.44 | 5.5% | -26.3% |
| Min FPS | 4.67 | 35.2% | 11.50 | 5.9% | +146.5% |

Median FPS remains visible in the raw CapFrameX captures, but it is not included in the across-run breakdown.

## Plain-language comparison

- **Overall speed:** Citron averaged **105.8 FPS**, compared with **128.2 FPS** for Adrenaline — **17.4% lower**.
- **1% lows:** Citron averaged **47.0 FPS** against Adrenaline's **47.7 FPS** — only **1.4% lower**.
- **0.1% lows:** Citron averaged **12.4 FPS**, **26.3% lower** than Adrenaline. This is the clearest weakness in the low-frame-rate results.
- **Average FPS consistency:** Citron's CV was **1.4%**, compared with **5.3%** for Adrenaline. That means Citron's results varied about **73% less between separate captures**, relative to their averages.
- **1% low consistency:** Citron's CV was **2.4%**, compared with **16.3%** for Adrenaline — about **86% less relative variation** between captures.
- **Minimum FPS:** The mean of the per-run minimum readings was **11.5 FPS** for Citron and **4.7 FPS** for Adrenaline. This is a single-frame extreme and should not outweigh the average and low-percentile results.

**In simple terms:** Adrenaline was faster overall. Citron's average FPS and 1% lows were more repeatable from run to run, but its 0.1% lows were weaker. CV describes variation between separate captures; it does not directly measure stutter within an individual capture.
